About

Lior Gal, CPA (Adv.)

CPA and Attorney with ten years' experience in international taxation, corporate restructuring, and compliance — built inside the Israel Tax Authority and at Meitar Law Offices, where he advised multinational and high-tech clients on cross-border transactions, transfer pricing, and complex restructurings.
